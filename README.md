# Turn any PDF into a podcast with NotebookLM

Free. Works in your phone or laptop browser. Two AI hosts talk through your document like a podcast.

Note: on 16 July 2026 Google renamed NotebookLM to **Gemini Notebook**. Same tool, and the old address still works.

## The 5-step setup

1. **Open it.** Go to notebooklm.google.com and sign in with any Google account.
2. **Make a notebook.** Create a new notebook and upload your sources: a PDF, your notes, a report. One good source beats ten random ones.
3. **Find the Audio Overview.** In the **Studio** panel, choose **Audio Overview**. Before generating, open its customise options.
4. **Set it up.**
   - **Format:** Deep Dive (two hosts, in depth), The Brief (one voice, under two minutes), The Critique (feedback on your work) or The Debate (two sides).
   - **Length:** Shorter, Default or Longer (Longer is English only).
   - **Language:** pick yours from 80+ languages.
   - **Prompt box:** paste one of the prompts below.
5. **Generate and listen.** Start it. It can take a few minutes and keeps running if you do something else. Play it in the app, change the speed, or tap the download icon to listen offline.

Bonus: choose **Interactive mode** before generating, then tap **Join** while it plays to ask the hosts a question (English only).

## 3 prompts for a better overview

Paste one into the prompt box in step 4.

**1. Teach me from zero**
```
Explain this for a smart beginner. Define every key term the first time it appears, use one everyday example per big idea, and end with the 5 points I must remember.
```

**2. Focus on what matters**
```
Skip the background and the methods. Spend the time on the main argument, the strongest evidence for it, and the 3 most useful takeaways. Point out anything surprising or counter-intuitive.
```

**3. Quiz me**
```
After each section, pause and ask me one question about it, then give the answer and why. Finish with a quick recap of everything covered.
```

## Tips

- Your sources stay the only source: if it is not in the document, the hosts should not say it. Still check key facts against the original.
- Too long? Choose The Brief or Shorter.
- Long book? Make one notebook per chapter for tighter episodes.
